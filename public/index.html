How It Works

Security at Every Step

Military-grade cryptography combined with transparent, verifiable voting processes.

🪪
Government ID Verification
Your Voter ID is verified against the official electoral registry. Only eligible, registered citizens can participate.
📱
OTP Authentication
A one-time verification code is sent to your registered phone or email. Two-layer security before you can vote.
👤
Complete Anonymity
Your identity is never stored with your vote. The ballot is cryptographically separated from who cast it.
⛓️
Immutable Hash Chain
Every vote is added to a SHA-256 hash chain. Any tampering breaks the chain — publicly detectable by anyone.
🧾
Vote Receipt
After voting, receive a unique receipt token. Use it anytime to verify your vote was counted without revealing your choice.
📢
Party Transparency
Read party manifestos, review key decisions, and watch public speeches — make an informed choice before you vote.
