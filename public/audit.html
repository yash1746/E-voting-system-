Cryptographic Audit Trail

Anyone can verify the integrity of votes. Every vote is linked by SHA-256 hashes forming a tamper-evident chain. If even one vote is altered, the chain breaks and becomes visible here.

ℹ️
How to verify
Each vote contains the hash of the previous vote. If a hash doesn't match its predecessor, the chain is broken — indicating tampering. Ballot content is not shown here to preserve voter anonymity.
